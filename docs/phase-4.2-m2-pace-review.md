## Movement contract

Ordinary keyboard and touch-path movement, including the final on-foot Map approach, now use **22 world units per second** in every direction. Artwork stride, sprite scale, camera zoom, viewport and Appearance cannot change that speed. A straight 96-unit route takes 4.364 simulation seconds. Acceptance requires **96 unobstructed units within 5,000 wall-clock milliseconds**, with no more than **20% duration spread** across four facings in the same production episode. Obstructed/detoured routes retain their actual path length; the five-second threshold is not a promise through buildings or across a paused phone screen.

The engine still normalizes diagonal input, clamps frame delta to 50ms and optional speed to 0–230, uses its existing collision footprint and bounded A* pathfinding, and clears queued routes on manual interruption. Explicit long-distance Map navigation retains its labelled 230-unit/s segment until 96 path units remain. Ordinary movement uses nonpersistent renderer frame intervals, capped at 100ms and divided into collision steps of at most 50ms (at most 1.1 units per step at speed 22). This avoids losing navigation distance to smoothed/clamped render deltas on slower frames. Existing consumers and fast Map mode retain legacy timing. Clock/battery still tick once with the original 50ms clamp; no server clock, presence, durable state or new game-life timeline is introduced. Severe stalls intentionally do not produce unbounded catch-up. Pure regressions verify equal old/new elapsed time, battery and phone pause, plus bounded stall movement and collider safety. Nearby taps up to 128 units use a direct segment only when every sample (at most four units apart) is clear at the stricter existing A* 18-unit margin. This prevents an unobstructed short tap from overshooting the final cell centre and turning backward for a last-pixel correction. Obstructed and long paths retain the original A* search; corner-cutting remains rejected. No position/frame setter or test-only velocity is introduced.

Animation remains contact-distance driven: eight approved walking poses per projected cycle, `floor(distance / (projectedStride[facing] * spriteScale) * 8) % 8`. The original four-facing strides remain 30.72 vertically and 64 laterally; these now affect **pose cadence only**, never locomotion speed. At the default native 360×800 production canvas (731px, scale about 0.370), the resulting cadence is approximately **15.5 poses/s vertically and 7.4 poses/s laterally**. This is temporal resampling of the approved planted-foot/recoil/toe-off sequence, not an injected running sequence, fixed timer animation or extra animation renderer. Cadence changes with visual scale to retain source-foot displacement. All eight phases must be observed during each measured walk; a blocked/stopped player idles and retains facing. Reduced motion holds frame zero while preserving the same physical movement pace. Reviewer assessment of gait and foot fidelity remains necessary.
